Supervised Conform Ingest development in Hiero/Nuke Studio. Conforms plates, offline edits, and non-plate collaterals (per-plate CDL grades, per-shot LUTs, references). Automatically calculates full frame ranges, cut ranges, and head/tail handles, injecting verified metadata directly into ShotGrid during conform.
